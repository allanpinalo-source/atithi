Our story

Atithi means guest.

Our name comes from the old saying Atithi Devo Bhava, "the guest is god". That's why our logo shows two hands joined in welcome, and it's how we look after every table.

Our kitchen cooks Indian, Continental, Chinese, Thai and Zambian food in one garden, from tandoor-fired tikkas and butter chicken to T-bone steak with nshima and Zambezi bream.
